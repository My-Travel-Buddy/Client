# My Travel Buddy — Team Norms

## Working Hours

The team agrees on shared working hours and communicates availability changes early.

## Communication

- Use the agreed team channel
- Respond to blockers promptly
- Raise concerns early
- Keep the TA informed

## Standup

Each person answers:

- What did I complete?
- What will I do next?
- What exactly is blocking me?

## Pull Requests

- No direct pushes to `main`
- One approving review required
- Small PRs preferred
- Authors include testing instructions

## Decision Making

1. Discuss the issue.
2. Check the PRD, API contract, or database source of truth.
3. If unresolved, ask the TA.
4. Document the final decision.

## Conflict Resolution

- Address the work, not the person
- Be specific
- Avoid silent disagreement
- Escalate recurring blockers early

## Deployment Owners

Assign one owner for:

- Vercel
- Render
- Neon

The team uses one shared deployment for each service.

## Demo Responsibilities

Assign:

- Problem and product speaker
- Live demo speaker
- Technical architecture speaker
- Backup demo operator
